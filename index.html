Interactive Demo

Track your eye movement directly in the browser.

This demo uses WebGazer.js to estimate where you are looking. Press Start tracking and watch the live indicator and activity log react as your gaze moves across the page.

Your webcam never leaves the page and nothing is stored remotely.

Overview

The tracker estimates your gaze position from the webcam feed, then maps that point onto this page. As you look around, the glowing indicator follows and the log records which content region you linger on.

Workflow

  1. Allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Glance at each part of the page so the model can adapt to you.
  3. Watch the log update as you focus on different sections.

Tips for steadier results

  • Keep your head still and centered in the frame.
  • Make sure your face is evenly lit.
  • Use a laptop or external webcam at eye level.

Privacy

All processing happens in your browser. Webcam frames and gaze coordinates stay local; refreshing the page clears the calibration and activity log.
